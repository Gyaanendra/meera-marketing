# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hypotenuse Analytics internal teams, partners, and prospective candidates who need strategic alignment on decisions, standups, and historical context.

## Product Purpose

Meera is the AI Chief of Staff — it joins meetings, transcribes in real-time, extracts decisions and action items, and keeps teams aligned automatically. This marketing site sells the vision and drives sign-ups.

## Brand Personality

- **Bold** — unapologetic dark canvas, single accent color carries conviction
- **Quiet confidence** — no animations for the sake of animation; every motion earns its place
- **Precise** — sharp typography, tight spacing, zero visual noise

## Anti-references

- Cream/sand/beige body backgrounds (the saturated AI default)
- Gradient text and glassmorphism cards
- Generic SaaS hero with metric + label + CTA pattern
- Identical card grids with rounded corners
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section
- Numbered section markers (01, 02, 03) as decorative scaffolding
- Space Grotesk as the "it's technical" default font

## Design Principles

1. **Dark is the surface, not the aesthetic.** Use near-black backgrounds so the brand color and typography carry all the weight.
2. **One accent, fully committed.** A single teal reads as deliberate and confident; split accents read as undesigning.
3. **Motion is punctuation, not decoration.** Every entrance animation must communicate something. A counter counting to 100 means precision. A curtain rising means revelation.
4. **Typography is the design system at this scale.** One display family, one body family, no more. Weight and size contrast do the work that color and font variety cannot.
5. **Contrast is non-negotiable.** Body text must hit ≥7:1. If a shade feels "elegant" but is hard to read, it is wrong.
